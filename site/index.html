How it all fits together

STEMEd+ Commons runs on Knowledge Commons. The open resources help you find what you need; your free account lets you build projects and relationships; and everything you share lands in the STEMEd+ Library with a DOI, where others can find it in turn.

This site is part of STEMEd+ Commons, a Commons within Knowledge Commons. STEMEd+ Commons brings together STEM+ education communities to collectively build research insights and teaching innovations across disciplines. As an open access platform, STEMEd+ Commons helps ensure STEM+ education research and practice is accessible for all people, in all communities. This material is based upon work supported by the National Science Foundation under grants OAC-2226271, DRL-2321946, OAC-2531819 and by Michigan State University through the Jenison Fund.
